# Contributing to China Tech

**English** | [简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.zh-CN.md)

Thanks for helping developers discover technologies from China's developer ecosystem.

## How to contribute

- **Suggest a technology:** use the [suggestion form](https://github.com/zhongguo-dev/china-tech/issues/new?template=suggest-technology.yml).
- **Suggest a Radar development:** use the [Radar suggestion form](https://github.com/zhongguo-dev/china-tech/issues/new?template=suggest-radar-development.yml) for a specific new release, technical change, or engineering announcement.
- **Correct or remove a listing:** use the [correction form](https://github.com/zhongguo-dev/china-tech/issues/new?template=correction-or-removal.yml).
- **Improve repository documentation:** open a pull request.

Check the [Zhongguo.dev Directory](https://www.zhongguo.dev/directory) and existing issues before submitting to avoid duplicates. GitHub issues are public. Do not include confidential material or personal contact information.

## What makes a useful suggestion?

Provide the official name, official website or repository, an explanation of its relevance to developers, and reliable evidence connecting it to China's technology ecosystem. Official project documentation, original repositories, research papers, and first-party releases are especially useful.

Chinese-language content, a Chinese-language name, a popularity metric, or a high star count alone is not sufficient to establish origin. Commercial and open-source projects are both eligible if they meet the [editorial policy](EDITORIAL_POLICY.md).

The suggested topic is a starting point for review, not a final classification. An entry does not require a GitHub repository to be considered.

## Review process

Maintainers review suggestions for relevance, identity, evidence, duplicates, and accurate terminology. Accepted entries appear in the curated Zhongguo.dev Directory first; the GitHub index follows its published content. Suggestions may be declined or deferred with a brief explanation.

Submitting an issue or pull request does not guarantee inclusion. Community suggestions do not automatically modify the catalog.

## China Dev Radar suggestions

Radar suggestions are leads for possible future coverage, not verified developments or automatic publications. Include a dated original announcement when possible, explain what changed and its relevance to developers, and provide first-party evidence. Maintainers assess relevance, dates, duplicates, and claims independently.

The public archive contains only published Radar editions. Suggestions, discussion comments, or contributor recommendations do not automatically become Radar articles.

## Pull requests

Keep pull requests focused and explain the change, with links to relevant issues and first-party sources for factual claims. Avoid promotional language, unsupported rankings, and unverified translations.

Generated catalog sections are maintained from the published Directory. Please propose content corrections through an issue rather than editing those sections directly.

We welcome respectful discussion and constructive disagreement. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
