# Community guidelines

China Tech welcomes developers, maintainers, researchers, and readers from all backgrounds. Contributions should help others discover and understand technologies accurately.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Discuss technical evidence and claims rather than attacking people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when recommending your own work.
- Respect copyright, privacy, and the differing perspectives of contributors.
- Accept good-faith corrections and editorial decisions professionally.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, personal attacks, spamming, misleading promotion, impersonation, publication of private information, and deliberately falsified sources are not permitted.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove inappropriate content and may restrict participation when necessary. Use GitHub's reporting features for platform-policy violations. For issues that can safely be discussed publicly, open a focused repository issue. Do not post sensitive reports or personal information in public discussions.
